Getting to central Akita

By trainTokyo → Kakunodate
Akita Shinkansen, about 3 hours. Covered by the JR Pass.
By airHaneda → Odate-Noshiro
About 1 hr 10 min with ANA. Shared taxis connect to Mt. Moriyoshi.
Local railwayAkita Nairiku Line
Runs from Kakunodate to Takanosu. The JR Pass is not valid on this line.

Tip: trains and shared taxis in rural Akita are infrequent. Check timetables and book ahead before you go.
